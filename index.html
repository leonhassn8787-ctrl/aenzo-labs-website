Quick answers

Before we get started

Which types of projects do you support?

We discuss Web3, crypto, AI, DeFi, wallets, payments, RWA and other technology projects based on fit and campaign requirements.

How do I request a campaign?

Message our team with your project name, website, target geography, goals, timeline and the services you are considering.

Are campaign results guaranteed?

No. Deliverables and reporting can be agreed in advance, but reach, signups, investment and financial outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Can I request only one service?

Yes. You can discuss an individual service or a broader campaign based on your needs and budget.
